LumaGrader Photo Studio
=======================

An offline photo organiser and RAW editor for macOS.
Needs macOS 14 Sonoma or newer, on an Apple silicon or Intel Mac.


1. INSTALL

   Drag LumaGrader Photo Studio into the Applications folder in this window.
   Then eject the "LumaGrader Photo Studio" disk (in the Finder sidebar).


2. OPEN IT THE FIRST TIME  (a warning here is normal)

   The first time you open LumaGrader, macOS blocks it with a message like
   "Apple could not verify LumaGrader Photo Studio is free of malware".
   THIS IS NORMAL.

   Why: macOS only opens apps without asking when they are signed with a
   paid Apple Developer ID and checked ("notarized") by Apple. LumaGrader is
   made by an independent developer and shared directly, not through the
   App Store, so it isn't notarized. Any app shared this way gets the same
   warning. You confirm it once in System Settings; after that LumaGrader
   opens normally.

   LumaGrader works fully offline: no account, nothing is uploaded, and your
   original photo files are never changed.

   macOS 15 Sequoia and newer:
     a. Open LumaGrader from Applications. macOS says it "could not verify"
        the app. Click Done (do NOT move it to the Trash).
     b. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
     c. Scroll down. Next to "LumaGrader Photo Studio was
        blocked…", click Open Anyway.
     d. Enter your password, then click Open Anyway again.

   macOS 14 Sonoma:
     Right-click (or Control-click) LumaGrader in Applications, choose Open,
     then click Open.

   If macOS says LumaGrader "is damaged":
     That's the same check, worded differently. Open Terminal and paste:

       xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/LumaGrader Photo Studio.app"

     Press Return, then open LumaGrader normally.

   Still stuck? Email support@lumagrader.app

   Inside LumaGrader, Help > LumaGrader Guide explains every feature step by
   step, and has these security steps too (Mac Security tab).


3. YOUR PHOTOS AND CATALOG

   LumaGrader never changes your original photo files. Ratings, labels,
   edits, collections and people are kept in a catalog at:

     ~/Library/Application Support/Darkroom/Catalog.sqlite

   File > Catalog Backup lets you back up the catalog now, turn on a
   daily backup (the newest 10 copies are kept), choose a backup folder
   on another drive, or restore an earlier copy.


4. UPDATE

   Install a newer LumaGrader the same way, and choose Replace when asked.
   Your catalog, settings and presets stay as they are.


5. UNINSTALL

   Move LumaGrader from Applications to the Trash. To remove its data too,
   also delete the folder ~/Library/Application Support/Darkroom
   (this deletes your catalog and its backups, not your photos).
